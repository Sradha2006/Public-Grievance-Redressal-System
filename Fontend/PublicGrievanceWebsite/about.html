About Our Public Grievance Redressal System

A digital platform designed to make the complaint process easier, faster, transparent and more accessible for citizens.

What Is This Project?

The Public Grievance Redressal System is a web-based application that allows citizens to register complaints related to public services and track their progress online. It helps departments receive, manage and resolve complaints systematically.

Why Is It Needed?

Traditional complaint processes can involve paperwork, repeated visits to government offices and difficulty getting updates. This system provides a centralized digital platform to reduce these difficulties.

Our Objectives

  • Make complaint registration simple and accessible.
  • Improve communication between citizens and departments.
  • Provide complaint tracking and status updates.
  • Support systematic complaint management.
  • Encourage transparency and accountability.

How Does It Work?

  1. Registration: Citizens create an account.
  2. Login: Registered citizens log in to the portal.
  3. Complaint Submission: Citizens submit their grievances.
  4. Department Assignment: Complaints are assigned to the concerned department.
  5. Status Tracking: Citizens can check complaint progress.
  6. Resolution: The department updates the complaint status after action is taken.

Key Features

  • Citizen Registration and Login
  • Online Complaint Submission
  • Complaint Tracking
  • Department Assignment
  • Admin Dashboard
  • Complaint Status Management
  • Resolution Reports

Benefits

  • Saves citizens' time and effort.
  • Reduces dependency on paperwork.
  • Makes complaint progress easier to monitor.
  • Helps departments organize complaints.
  • Improves accessibility to grievance services.

Future Scope

In the future, the system can be enhanced with SMS and email notifications, complaint priority management, document uploads, multilingual support, mobile applications and advanced analytics.
