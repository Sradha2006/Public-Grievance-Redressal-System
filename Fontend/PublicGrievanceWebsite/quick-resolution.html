Quick Complaint Resolution

Our process for handling and resolving public complaints.

1. Complaint Registration

Citizens submit their complaints through our online portal.

2. Department Assignment

Complaints are assigned to the relevant department for review.

3. Complaint Review

The concerned department examines the complaint and takes necessary action.

4. Status Updates

Citizens can track the progress and receive complaint updates.

5. Resolution

After the issue is addressed, the complaint status is updated.
