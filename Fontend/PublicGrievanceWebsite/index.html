About Us

The Public Grievance Redressal System is a citizen-friendly online platform developed to make the complaint process simple, transparent and accessible. It allows citizens to register complaints regarding public issues, track complaint status and receive updates on their progress. The system assigns complaints to the appropriate departments, helping authorities manage and resolve issues efficiently. Our aim is to improve communication between citizens and authorities, promote accountability and contribute to better public services.
